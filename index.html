01 / FINANCIAL CALENDAR

Indian FY standard

Every record is mapped to 1 April–31 March of its FY. Financial quarters: Q1 Apr–Jun, Q2 Jul–Sep, Q3 Oct–Dec, Q4 Jan–Mar. The FY2026–27 dataset includes Apr–Oct 2026 only; no invented future actuals.

02 / REVENUE REALIZATION

Net Sales (₹)

Gross sales − regular trade discount − total promotional spend. Regular discount and scheme spend are separately tracked to avoid double counting.

03 / PROFIT AFTER SCHEME

Net Incremental Profit (₹)

[(Actual units − baseline units) × pre-scheme unit contribution] − scheme spend. Pre-scheme unit contribution = list price − regular discount per unit − manufacturing cost per unit − distribution cost per unit.

04 / CAMPAIGN RETURN

Weighted Scheme ROI (%)

100 × SUM(net incremental profit) ÷ SUM(scheme spend). Do not average row-level ROI. A positive ROI implies modeled incremental gain exceeds trade spend, not necessarily causal attribution.

05 / VOLUME LIFT

Promotional Unit Uplift (%)

100 × [SUM(actual units) − SUM(baseline units)] ÷ SUM(baseline units). Baseline units are synthetic counterfactual estimates, not measured untreated-control outcomes.

06 / EXECUTIVE CONTROL

Spend, margin & budget

Budget utilization = SUM(scheme spend) ÷ SUM(scheme budget). Contribution margin = SUM(contribution) ÷ SUM(net sales). Scheme contribution = net sales − manufacturing COGS − distribution cost.

07 / YOY GOVERNANCE

Like-for-like prior FY

FY comparisons use matching fiscal months and preserve the current non-year slicers. FY2026–27 (Apr–Oct) is compared with Apr–Oct FY2025–26, never a full preceding FY.

08 / IMPORTANT QUALIFIER

Synthetic / illustrative data

All 6,192 observations are reproducibly generated for portfolio demonstration and must not be interpreted as company sales, real customer records, verified uplift estimates, tax invoices or audited forecasts. INR prices exclude any GST modelling.
